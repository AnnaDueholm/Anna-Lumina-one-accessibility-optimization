Product information

This speaker combines Scandinavian elegance with rich, powerful audio, the LUMINA One is designed for a lifestyle where music brings people together. Featuring smooth, rounded corners, an elegant soft-touch handle for effortless carrying, and a compact profile standing at 30 cm high and 20 cm wide, it is lightweight and beautifully crafted. It's the ultimate companion for every shared moment.

Key elements
  • Immersive Sound: Up to 100 dB of clear, dynamic audio for any outdoor hangout.
  • Nordic Design & Lighting: Curved edges, fabric mesh, and customizable LED modes (Chill, Party, Sunset). Available in Beige, Silver, Black, and Brown.
  • Portable & Compact: 30 x 20 cm, 4.5 kg, with a built-in handle and matching tote bag.
  • All-Day Battery: Up to 18 hours of playback with fast USB-C charging.
  • Seamless Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3, AUX, and multi-speaker pairing (up to 4 units).
  • Social Connect: Share and blend playlists with friends via the app.
  • Weather-Resistant: IPX5 rating against rain and splashes.

Features

Sound

The Lumina One combines powerful acoustic drivers with advanced DSP tuning to deliver clean, dynamic sound at any volume. Its dual passive radiators enhance low-end depth, creating a full and punchy bass response without compromising clarity. With Bluetooth 5.3, it ensures a fast and stable wireless connection for seamless audio streaming.

Design

The Lumina One features a sleek, minimalist Scandinavian design that blends effortlessly into any space. Its smooth contours and premium materials give it a modern, timeless look that feels both elegant and durable. With customizable ambient lighting, it adds a warm, atmospheric touch to any setting.

Power

The Lumina One offers up to 18 hours of playtime, giving you reliable, all-day performance without frequent recharging. Its efficient power management ensures consistent audio quality from the first song to the last. Whether you're out for a picnic, at the beach, or relaxing at home, the long-lasting battery keeps your music going wherever you are.

Social connect

The Lumina One's Social Connect feature lets you pair multiple speakers together for a richer, more immersive sound experience. With just a single tap, friends can join in and connect their devices, making it easy to share playlists and create a collective vibe. Whether you're hosting a party or hanging out in a small group, Social Connect keeps everyone involved in the music.
